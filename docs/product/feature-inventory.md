# NextSet prioritised feature inventory

Status: logging-first MVP boundary accepted 2026-09-07 (`D-009`); implementation and detailed design remain unapproved  
Last updated: 2026-09-07  
Promise: **start a workout, record your sets and see what changes over time**

## 1. How to read this inventory

This is a product decision record, not a competitor checklist. Each item has one current phase and one stable ID. IDs retain their original prefix after a move; the section placement and explicit current phase govern, not an old `MVP` or `FND` prefix. “Foundation” means enabling capability that must exist before the MVP can be trusted; it is not necessarily visible. “Excluded” means outside the present product boundary, while “rejected” means the concept conflicts with a documented principle and should not be reopened without materially new evidence.

Audience shorthand: **N** new gym users, **I** intermediate, **E** experienced lifters, **G** general fitness, **S** schedule-variable. Complexity is relative (**S/M/L/XL**) and includes product, design, content, domain, data and test work—not just code.

Measurement references `PRD-SM-*` in [`product-requirements.md`](product-requirements.md). “Direct” promise alignment means the feature directly reduces friction in starting, recording, repeating or understanding recorded progress; “Enabler” means it makes that promise reliable or safe; “Adjacent” means useful but not central.

### Accepted first-release boundary

A user can start a blank workout, log it, finish, review history and see meaningful trends forever without a goal, routine, programme or schedule. Repeat and optional saved routines make later visits faster. No scheduled-next-workout, carry-forward, short-mode or progression control belongs in the default product. Manual exercise changes remain available during logging.

The foundation prototype demonstrates selected interactions with illustrative or in-session records. Its README and QA evidence define actual implemented measurement modes, persistence and test coverage; this inventory describes the future first release, not a claim of completed production functionality.

## 7. Rejected after foundation research and product assessment

These are normative product rejections grounded in the brief's user-control, usefulness and wellbeing constraints and the foundation research package. The research found the strongest opening to be a trustworthy local-first journal with fast repeated entry, flexible programme execution and explainable progression; its highest-risk pains are data loss, logging friction, rigid programmes, opaque advice and portability. It found social/streak harm plausible but not yet quantified, so rejection here rests on both the product boundary and precaution against optimising engagement over the central promise. See [`../research/competitor-analysis.md`](../research/competitor-analysis.md) (“Market shape”) and [`../research/user-pain-points.md`](../research/user-pain-points.md) (“Ranked pain-point inventory” and evidence limitations).

## 8. Cross-phase guardrails

1. No future or excluded item may create a hidden MVP dependency.
2. A feature cannot move into MVP until it maps to a `PRD-FR-*` requirement, normative domain rules, a complete `JNY-*` journey, failure recovery and automated acceptance evidence.
3. Guidance requires the classification and claim boundary in `WPR-CLASS-*` and `SAF-*`; “smart” or “AI” is not a waiver.
4. Content-heavy features require a named maintenance owner, provenance, licence and update process before release.
5. Engagement is never a sufficient measure where data control, logging accuracy, accessibility, privacy or wellbeing worsens.
6. Phase changes belong in the decision log and require product-owner approval when they alter the MVP boundary.

## 9. Remaining product decisions

- Scope is accepted; final Tempo Ledger treatment, revised critical journeys and architecture await owner review of the updated prototype.
- D-012 and D-013 accept the initial 47-movement built-in catalogue, grouped in the picker by Chest & shoulders, Back, Legs, Arms, and Bodyweight & core, with search, category shortcuts and expandable sections. Custom exercise modes and unit/laterality coverage still require production validation.
- The initial trend questions are accepted. Exact display windows and comparison labels require comprehension testing; observed PR categories are proposed in `PRD-FR-026`. Estimated strength/volume scoring and richer analytics remain POST.
- No analytics collection is assumed. Use moderated research or explicitly consented local beta instrumentation.
- No routine, programme, recommendation or deferred feature may be required to access history/trends. A blank-workout-only user has the complete v1 experience.
